MDP & Quaternions (Theory)

Formulating the State Space mathematically.

Reinforcement Learning relies on the formalism of the Markov Decision Process (MDP), defined by the tuple \( (S, A, P, R, \gamma) \). In this lecture, we focus on \( S \), the State Space. A core assumption of the MDP is the Markov Property: the current state contains all necessary information to make an optimal decision.

Topology and Gimbal Lock

Why use quaternions instead of Euler angles (pitch, roll, yaw)? Euler angles suffer from singularities (Gimbal Lock) where the Jacobian matrix loses rank, dropping a degree of freedom when pointing straight up or down. The physics engine utilizes Quaternions (4D hypercomplex numbers) to guarantee continuous, singularity-free integration over time. We extract Euler yaw only at the final boundary before network inference.

Mathematical Formulation

A quaternion represents rotation using four numbers: W, X, Y, Z. It is mathematically immune to Gimbal Lock, but it's difficult for an AI to parse into a planar driving direction. Therefore, we mathematically convert the 4D quaternion back into a simple 2D "Yaw" angle using the Atan2 function.
