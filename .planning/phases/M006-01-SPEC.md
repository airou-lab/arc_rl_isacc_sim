## 1. Objective & Literature Foundation

Enable command-conditioned multi-branch navigation (`turn_token in {-1.0 (Left), 0.0 (Straight), +1.0 (Right)}`) across the full 3-loop USD track (`openStreetUSD/no_graph_sim_clean_1x.usda`) and its 4-way intersections (`junction_55` at `(-15.9710, -6.8750)`, `junction_18` at `(-15.9694, +6.8750)`, and Sector 4 C2 intersection corridor at `(-5.50, -7.10)`), grounded in **Conditional Imitation / Command-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning** (Codevilla et al., ICRA 2018; Levine et al., JMLR 2016 Spatial Softmax) and the project's hierarchical `WorkerNode` / `IntersectionGraph` specification (`arcproLab/policy_stack/agent/`).

### Critical Zero-Regression Invariant
While background 48-environment training finishes baking the `M005` single-loop visual representation baseline toward 500k+ steps, **default single-loop mode (`randomize_turn_tokens=False`) must remain 100% bit-exact** with existing `M005` behavior and pass all 6 forensic audit test suites plus `test_track_topology_invariants.py`, `test_cornering_remediation.py`, and `verify_rerouted_track.py`.

### Task `t-1-track-manager` — Gate 1: Multi-Branch Splines & 3-Branch Tensor Bank
- **Target File:** `arcproLab/mdp/track_manager.py`
- **Symbols Modified/Added:**
  - `TrackManager.__init__`, `TrackManager.ensure_synced`
  - `TrackManager._build_multi_branch_bank`
  - `TrackManager.resolve_branch_ids`
  - `TrackManager.compute_errors`
  - `TrackManager.get_lookahead_curvature`
  - `TrackManager.get_forward_waypoints`
- **Specification:**
  1. Preserve `self.waypoints` `(5000, 3)` and `self.curvature_tensor` `(5000,)` intact for `branch_id = 0` (`turn_token = -1.0`, Left turn circuit loop).
  2. Pre-materialize `self.branch_waypoints` of shape `(3, 5000, 3)`, `self.branch_waypoints_xy` of shape `(3, 5000, 2)`, `self.branch_curvatures` of shape `(3, 5000)`, and `self.branch_seg_bounds` of shape `(3, 5000, 2)` (storing `[seg_start, seg_end]` per waypoint so lookahead window indexing clamps cleanly at open branch ends without wrapping across disjoint segments):
     - **`branch_id = 0` (`LEFT`, `turn_token = -1.0`)**: Canonical 5,000-WP counter-clockwise circuit (`self.waypoints`).
     - **`branch_id = 1` (`STRAIGHT`, `turn_token = 0.0` at intersection)**: 5,000 waypoints partitioned into three 1.028cm-spaced straight-through intersection corridors (`kappa = 0.0`):
       - Segment S-A (`0..1699`, `1,700` WPs): West Runway & `junction_55` Straight Southbound (`X = -16.205`, `Y = +5.50 -> -11.98`, `wp_yaw = +pi/2`, `kappa = 0.0`).
       - Segment S-B (`1700..3399`, `1,700` WPs): South Straightaway & Sector 4 / C2 Straight Eastbound (`Y = -7.100`, `X = -15.30 -> +2.18`, `wp_yaw = pi`, `kappa = 0.0`).
       - Segment S-C (`3400..4999`, `1,600` WPs): Top North Straightaway & `junction_18` Straight Westbound (`Y = +7.100`, `X = -6.00 -> -22.45`, `wp_yaw = 0.0`, `kappa = 0.0`).
     - **`branch_id = 2` (`RIGHT`, `turn_token = +1.0` at intersection)**: 5,000 waypoints partitioned into three 1.028cm-spaced 90° right-turn intersection corridors (`R = 0.50 m`, `kappa = -2.0 m^-1 < 0` in the turn arc):
       - Segment R-A (`0..1699`, `1,700` WPs): West Runway Southbound (`X = -16.205, Y = +5.50 -> -6.156`) -> 90° Right Turn at `junction_55` (`R = 0.50 m`, center `(-16.705, -6.156)`, `kappa = -2.0 m^-1`) -> West Loop South Leg Westbound (`Y = -6.656, X = -16.705 -> -21.90`, `wp_yaw = 0.0`).
       - Segment R-B (`1700..3399`, `1,700` WPs): South Straightaway Eastbound (`Y = -7.100, X = -15.30 -> -5.50`) -> 90° Right Turn at Sector 4 / C2 (`R = 0.50 m`, center `(-5.50, -7.600)`, `kappa = -2.0 m^-1`) -> Southbound Exit (`X = -5.00, Y = -7.600 -> -14.49`, `wp_yaw = +pi/2`).
       - Segment R-C (`3400..4999`, `1,600` WPs): Top North Straightaway Westbound (`Y = +7.100, X = -6.00 -> -15.252`) -> 90° Right Turn at `junction_18` (`R = 0.50 m`, center `(-15.252, +7.600)`, `kappa = -2.0 m^-1`) -> Outer Grand Loop North Branch Northbound (`X = -15.752, Y = +7.600 -> +14.01`, `wp_yaw = -pi/2`).
  3. Support optional `turn_token: torch.Tensor | None = None` and `branch_ids: torch.Tensor | None = None` in `compute_errors()`, `get_lookahead_curvature()`, and `get_forward_waypoints()`. Track `self.last_branch_ids` and `self.branch_switched_mask` so branch transitions immediately re-index via global branch search.

### Task `t-2-topology-permeability` — Gate 2: Multi-Branch Boundary Permeability & Intersection Envelopes
- **Target Files:** `arcproLab/mdp/track_topology.py`, `arcproLab/mdp/terminations.py`
- **Symbols Modified/Added:**
  - `in_junction_55_box`, `in_junction_18_box`, `in_multi_branch_intersection`
  - `in_branch_permeable_gate`
  - `in_off_cliff_east`, `in_wrong_lane_intersection`
  - `compute_step_road_state`
  - `white_line_contact`
- **Specification:**
  1. Maintain zero `isaaclab` or `omni` imports in `track_topology.py` and do not modify `track_boundaries_1x.npz` on disk.
  2. Add canonical spatial predicates in `track_topology.py`:
     - `in_junction_55_box(x, y)`: `(tx >= -17.50) & (tx <= -14.80) & (ty >= -8.50) & (ty <= -5.50)`
     - `in_junction_18_box(x, y)`: `(tx >= -17.50) & (tx <= -14.80) & (ty >= 5.50) & (ty <= 8.50)`
     - `in_multi_branch_intersection(x, y)`: `in_junction_55_box(x, y) | in_junction_18_box(x, y) | in_c2_turn(x, y) | in_intersection_expert_zone(x, y)`
     - `in_branch_permeable_gate(x, y, dist_g, turn_token=None, multi_branch_active=None)`: Always true when `in_stop_bar(x, y, dist_g)` is true; when `multi_branch_active` is true (or `turn_token > -0.5` inside `in_multi_branch_intersection(x, y)`), also grants white-line permeability across the loop's outer boundary crossing along the Straight (`branch_id = 1`) and Right (`branch_id = 2`) exit corridors while never relaxing yellow-line contact (`hit_yellow`) on solid medians.
  3. Upgrade `in_off_cliff_east(x, y, turn_token=None, multi_branch_active=None)` and `in_wrong_lane_intersection(x, y, turn_token=None, multi_branch_active=None)` with optional backward-compatible arguments so default `turn_token=None` / `turn_token=-1.0` is unchanged, while Straight (`0.0`, `Y in [-7.50, -6.50]`) and Right (`+1.0`, `X in [-6.00, -4.50], Y <= -6.80`) branches are exempted from the left-turn eastern cliff and North apex wrong-lane boxes.
  4. In `compute_step_road_state(env)`, execute `RoadManager.update(env)` **before** `tm.compute_errors()` and `tm.get_lookahead_curvature()`, passing `branch_ids=rm.get_active_branch_ids()` whenever `rm.is_multi_branch_active()` is true.
- **Verification Command:** `pytest tests/test_track_topology_invariants.py tests/test_cornering_remediation.py tests/test_multi_branch_gates.py -k "gate2 or topology or cornering"`

### Task `t-3-road-manager-mdp` — Gate 3: Vectorized `RoadManager` Turn-Token FSM & Token-Bound Rewards
- **Target Files:** `arcproLab/mdp/road_manager.py`, `arcproLab/mdp/rewards.py`, `arcproLab/mdp/events.py`
- **Symbols Modified/Added:**
  - `RoadManager.set_randomization`, `RoadManager.set_mission_tokens`, `RoadManager.randomize_missions`, `RoadManager.reset`, `RoadManager.update`, `RoadManager.get_active_branch_ids`, `RoadManager.is_multi_branch_active`
  - `waypoint_progress_reward`, `lateral_error_reward`, `boundary_penalty`, `lane_centering_reward`, `intersection_expert_reward`
  - `reset_robot_curriculum_spawn`
- **Specification:**
  1. In `RoadManager`:
     - Keep `self.randomize_turn_tokens = False` by default (unless `ARCPRO_RANDOMIZE_TURN_TOKENS=1` or `rm.set_randomization(True)` is called) so M005 single-loop training and existing tests remain deterministic (`turn_token = -1.0` in corners, `0.0` on straightaways, `active_branch_ids = 0`).
     - Support per-env `self.mission_tokens` (`(num_envs, num_agents)` in `{-1.0, 0.0, +1.0}`), `self.allowed_tokens` (for Stage 1/2/3 curriculum phasing), `self.fsm_state` (`0=CRUISING, 1=DECIDING, 2=COMMITTED`), `self.active_branch_ids` (`0=Left, 1=Straight, 2=Right`), `self.exit_correct` (`-1, 0, +1`), and `self.just_exited` (`bool`).
     - When `randomize_turn_tokens=True` or `set_mission_tokens()` is active: lock `turn_token` to `mission_tokens` upon entering `in_multi_branch_intersection(x, y)`, route `active_branch_ids` (`-1.0 -> 0`, `0.0 -> 1`, `+1.0 -> 2`), and detect exit completion.
  2. In `rewards.py`:
     - `lateral_error_reward`: Make inward apex slack sign-symmetric with curvature (`is_inward = torch.where(kappa < -1e-4, lat_err < 0.0, lat_err > 0.0)`).
     - `boundary_penalty`: Mask `too_close_w` with `in_branch_permeable_gate(x, y, dist_g, turn_token, multi_branch_active)`.
     - `lane_centering_reward`: Use centerline error (`2.0 * |lat_err|`) when `multi_branch_active` is active inside an intersection and pass `turn_token` to `in_wrong_lane_intersection`.
     - `waypoint_progress_reward`: Re-anchor `prev_wp_idx_reward = current_idx.clone()` for any environment where `env.extras.get("branch_switched_mask")` is True on that step so branch switches produce zero spurious delta spikes while progress along the branch accumulates at ~1 WP/cm.
  3. In `events.py`:
     - Hook `rm.reset(env_ids)` inside `reset_robot_curriculum_spawn()` alongside `GoSignalManager.reset(env_ids)`.
- **Verification Command:** `pytest tests/test_multi_branch_gates.py tests/test_track_topology_invariants.py tests/test_cornering_remediation.py`
